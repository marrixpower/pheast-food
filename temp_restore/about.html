ABOUT PH'EAST

ASIAN STREET FOOD. LOCAL SOUL.

FROM THE STREETS TO THE BATTERY

PH'EAST was born from a love of the hawker centers and night markets that line the streets of Singapore, Taipei, Bangkok, and Hong Kong. We wanted to recreate that electric atmosphere — where the air is thick with the aroma of sizzling woks, where strangers become friends over steaming bowls, and where every corner reveals a new flavor adventure.

Located in The Battery Atlanta, steps from Truist Park, PH'EAST brings together six of the most exciting Asian culinary concepts under one roof. Whether you're grabbing boba before a Braves game, settling in for a ramen feast after work, or exploring new flavors on a weekend date night — PH'EAST is your destination for authentic, affordable, and absolutely delicious Asian street food.

Our food hall is more than just a place to eat. It's a gathering place, a cultural experience, and a celebration of the incredible diversity of Asian cuisine. Come hungry, leave happy.
